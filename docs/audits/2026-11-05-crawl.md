## Top 10 issues by impact (hand-curated)

1. Character names doubled across the wiki roster: "Jim Jim", "Kevin Kevin", "Nathan Swift Swift", "Sam Sam" (40+ on `/wiki/chara`), and in slugs/titles too (`/wiki/chara/jim-jim`, `/wiki/chara/nathan-swift-swift`, `/wiki/chara/tod-ironside-tod`). Looks like nickname appended to full name without a dedupe.
2. `cdn.rosegriffon.fr/dx11/menu/...` returns 502 on GET and HEAD: 314 character face icons, broken on `/wiki/chara`, every `/wiki/chara/*` page and the wiki/news pages that embed them (141 pages).
3. `/iergday?campagne=inazuma-rg-2` references two raw Discord videos (28 MB `.mp4` + 27 MB `.mov`) at about 55 MB total. The `.mov` will not play in most browsers. These need a poster, `preload="none"` and a transcode.
4. `/storage/v1/object/public/assets/campagnes/iergday/*` returns 400 (`affiche-v2.webp`, `reglement-v2.webp`, Instagram thumbnails on `/feed?reseau=instagram`). The missing iergday poster probably feeds the 0.52 CLS baseline.
5. `/community?serveur=achillea` is 5.7 MB of HTML with 3144 `<img>` tags; `/community` and `?serveur=rose-griffon` are 3.5 MB with 1843. It needs pagination or virtualization (baseline perf 41).
6. Untranslated English on `/wiki/chara`: "Normal girl"/"Normal boy" and the element labels "mountain/wind/forest/fire" sit next to the French ones, plus "Inconnu Inconnu" and "Const : — —" placeholders. The `/wiki` command palette shows "Command Palette", "Search for a command to run...".
7. 93 pages have no canonical (all `/news/tweet/*`, `/wiki/avatar`, `/settings`) and 71 have a canonical that does not match the URL. `/settings` is crawlable and indexable even though it is a user page.
8. Duplicated visible labels: "Critique Critique" (`/news/critique-communautaire-...`, `/wiki`), "Bureau Bureau" (`/notre-equipe`), "Chroniques Chroniques" (`/wiki`), "Tournoi Tournoi" (`/achillea/tournaments`), "Staff Staff" / "Communauté Communauté" (`/community?vue=staff`). Badge text and heading are concatenated without a separator, or the label is actually repeated.
9. Titles too long on 169 pages: the wiki suffix `| Inazuma Eleven Victory Road - Azalée | Wiki Azalée | Rose Griffon` pushes `/wiki/niveau` to about 160 characters. 25 pages have no `h1`: `/achillea/tournaments/{7,9,19,21,22,23}`, `/wiki/avatar`, `/settings`. 36 descriptions are over 160 characters. Lengths include raw `&#x27;` entities, so they run about 5 characters high per apostrophe.
10. Slow server responses on `/feed?reseau=x&compte=*`: 12-52 s for the HTML (LEVEL5_times, AkihiroHino and InazumaCrossFR take about 52 s), and `/iergday?campagne=inazuma-rg-2` takes 10 s. Lighthouse on `/legal/cgu` gives perf 44, LCP 12.1 s and TBT 1.46 s with 135 requests: a static legal page ships the full app bundle (CSS 74 KB gz, 600 KB raw).

Also: GTM on all 400 pages. Only 1 `<img>` has no alt. 10.7k `<img>` have no width/height, mostly `next/image` fill, so this is lower risk. No mixed content. Only redirects: `/a-propos/soutenir` and `/contact` (308, single hop).

SUJETS

Posted on 11/22 (Sat) JST

Regarding the bugs in ver.1.3.0 (Nintendo Switch 2 / Nintendo Switch) and ver.1.3.1 (PlayStation®5 / PlayStation®4 / Steam / Xbox Series X|S)

Information on currently identified issues is provided below.

  • ・In Ranked and Lobby Matches, a starting lineup error can occur even with two or fewer HERO players, preventing the match from starting (ver.1.3.1 only).

▶ This issue is scheduled to be resolved in an update by Saturday morning, November 22 (JST).

[11/22（Saturday）18:00 Update]
Fixes have been made in version 1.3.2. For more details, please refer to the patch notes.

Annnouncement of ver.1.3.2 Release (PlayStation®5 / PlayStation®4 / Steam) Annnouncement of ver.1.3.2 Release (Xbox Series X|S)
  • ・Some save data is still affected by issues preventing progression. (ver.1.3.0 / ver.1.3.1)

▶ We are currently working to identify the cause and implement a fix to this issue as quickly as possible.

Other reported issues are still being looked into and will be fixed over time. We appreciate your patience in the meantime.

We sincerely apologize for the inconvenience and concern this has caused to our users. Thank you for your continued support of INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road.

INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road
500,000 Copies Sold Worldwide

LEVEL5 is proud to announce that cumulative worldwide sales of INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road have surpassed 500,000 copies across Nintendo Switch™ 2, Nintendo Switch™, PlayStation®5, PlayStation®4, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam®.

Over 500,000 Copies Sold Worldwide

INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road, which launched worldwide on Friday, November 14 (JST) as a digital-only title, has achieved strong post-launch sales, surpassing 500,000 digital copies sold worldwide as of November 20. The game has been extremely well received not only in Japan but also internationally across multiple digital storefronts. In Japan, it quickly reached No.1 in digital sales rankings, and on several overseas storefronts it has broken into the global Top 10. We are sincerely grateful for the incredible support from players all around the world.

This title’s endless Hyperdimensional Football RPG experience can be enjoyed through Story Mode, set 25 years after the original Inazuma Eleven game; Chronicle Mode, where players can relive the entire history of the series; and Kizuna Station, where they build their town and interact with friends online.
With the upcoming Galaxy, Ares and Orion Competition Routes in Chronicle Mode, as well as the launch of the Victory Road online tournament mode, players can look forward to countless hours of additional free content.

Follow & Repost Campaign Celebrating the Release

This campaign will award a total of 15 winners: ten will receive an original tumbler—five featuring Mark Evans (Mamoru Endo) and five featuring Destin Billows (Unmei Sasanami)—and five will receive an original towel featuring a special group illustration.
Participation is open to all fans. Simply follow the official account and repost the designated campaign post for a chance to win!

INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road —
The Latest Installment in the Series

In the latest entry in the Inazuma Eleven series, INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road, Story Mode follows a new protagonist, Destin Billows (Unmei Sasanami), on an all-new adventure.
In addition, Chronicle Mode allows players to scout over 5,400 characters from both past and present titles, while the online tournament mode, Victory Road, lets players compete against others from around the world.
The latest information is available on the official website and the official X.

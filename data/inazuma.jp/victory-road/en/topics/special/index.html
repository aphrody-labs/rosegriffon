2025.6.30 (Updated on 10.21) Rising High School Singer
Hinano Iwasaki Captivates with Her Clear Voice in the Ending Theme!

The ending theme "Guidepost" for the game INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road has been revealed!
Up-and-coming star Hinano Iwasaki makes her official debut as a singer with this ending theme and impresses with her crystal-clear voice.

In addition, the song was composed by Noriyasu Agematsu, known for his work on music for various anime and game titles.

Enjoy the powerful yet graceful voice of this young student, paired with visuals that capture the spirit of Inazuma's newest title.

2025.4.11 Choreographer akane joins as the voice of the café owner!
Also responsible for in-game dance choreography!Japanese Audio

Creator of the many energetic dance moves of avantgardey and more, choreographer akane lends her voice to café owner Olive Mapleton!
She brings a distinctive flair to "Café Tank," the charming hangout frequented by Destin and his friends!

On top of that, she choreographs the dances that appear in the game!

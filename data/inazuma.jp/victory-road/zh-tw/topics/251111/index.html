INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road
Kicks Off Early Access on November 11 (JST)

November 11, 2025 – LEVEL5 Inc. today announced that INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road—for Nintendo Switch™ 2, Nintendo Switch™, PlayStation®5, PlayStation®4, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam®—has begun Early Access on PlayStation®5 / PlayStation®4 / Xbox Series X|S and Advanced Access on Steam® as of today, Tuesday, November 11 (JST).

Deluxe Edition Bonus: Early access to the main game on select platforms.

Just before the official release date, players who purchase the Deluxe Edition will be able to play INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road early on PlayStation®5, PlayStation®4, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam®.
Additionally, Deluxe Edition players will be able to select one of eight Legendary Players and use them in Chronicle Mode and Competition Mode right from the start of the game.
They will also receive special Bond Objects to decorate their Bond Town, a Goal Celebration, and a special item that increases the amount of EXP earned.

Note Regarding the Day-1 Patch

A Day-1 Patch including several fixes and adjustments will be released on November 11, 2025 (JST).
It is strongly recommended that players install this patch before starting the game to ensure the best gameplay experience.

INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road —
The Latest Installment in the Series

In the latest entry in the Inazuma Eleven series, INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road, Story Mode follows a new protagonist, Destin Billows (Unmei Sasanami), on an all-new adventure.
In addition, Chronicle Mode allows players to scout over 5,400 characters from both past and present titles, while the online tournament mode, Victory Road, lets players compete against others from around the world.
The latest information is available on the official website and the official X.

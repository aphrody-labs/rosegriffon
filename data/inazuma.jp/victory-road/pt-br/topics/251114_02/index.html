INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road
Released on November 13, 2025 (GMT)

November 13, 2025 – LEVEL5 Inc. announced today that INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road is now available on Nintendo Switch™ 2, Nintendo Switch™, PlayStation®5, PlayStation®4, Xbox Series X|S, and Steam as of 3:00 PM (GMT).

INAZUMA ELEVEN:
Victory Road is out now –
endless fun awaits!

INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road is available starting today (November 13, 2025, GMT) .
The title delivers endless playability across multiple modes, including Story Mode, set 25 years after the original Inazuma Eleven game.
In Chronicle Mode, players can relive the entire history of the series, while Kizuna Station offers the ability to build their own town and spend time with friends online.
Countless hours of content await in this newly evolved Hyperdimensional Football RPG experience.

Story Mode –
First 30 Minutes of Gameplay Showcase

Gameplay footage featuring the opening segment of Story Mode has been released on YouTube.
The video includes on-screen text commentary that reflects the player's inner thoughts, offering an approachable and immersive experience.
Visuals have also been enhanced significantly from the demo version, making the showcase enjoyable for both newcomers and long-time fans of the series. This marks the beginning of a new era for Inazuma Eleven.

Follow & Repost Campaign Celebrating the Release

This campaign will award a total of 15 winners: ten will receive an original tumbler—five featuring Mark Evans (Mamoru Endo) and five featuring Destin Billows (Unmei Sasanami)—and five will receive an original towel featuring a special group illustration.
Participation is open to all fans.
Simply follow the official account and repost the designated campaign post for a chance to win!

How to Enter 1.Follow the official Inazuma Eleven X account.
2.Repost the designated campaign post.
Campaign Period Until Friday, November 21, 2025 at 2:59 PM (GMT)
Official X account

INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road —
The Latest Installment in the Series

In the latest entry in the Inazuma Eleven series, INAZUMA ELEVEN: Victory Road, Story Mode follows a new protagonist, Destin Billows (Unmei Sasanami), on an all-new adventure.
In addition, Chronicle Mode allows players to scout over 5,400 characters from both past and present titles, while the online tournament mode, Victory Road, lets players compete against others from around the world.
The latest information is available on the official website and the official X.
